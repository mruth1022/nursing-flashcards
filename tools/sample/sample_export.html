Sample Nursing Deck

Vital Signs (Adult)

Term

Definition / Details

Normal adult heart rate

60–100 bpm

  • Bradycardia: < 60 bpm
  • Tachycardia: > 100 bpm
  • Check apical pulse for a full minute before giving digoxin

Normal adult respiratory rate

12–20 breaths/min

Count for 30 seconds and double if regular;
a full minute if irregular.

Blood pressure categories (ACC/AHA 2017)

  1. Normal: < 120 / < 80 mmHg
  2. Elevated: 120–129 / < 80
  1. Stage 1: 130–139 / 80–89
  2. Stage 2: ≥ 140 / ≥ 90

Source: AHA guideline

Normal oral temperature

About 36.5–37.5 °C (97.7–99.5 °F). Fever is generally ≥ 38.0 °C (100.4 °F).

Normal SpO₂

95–100% on room air. For many COPD patients the target is 88–92% to avoid suppressing hypoxic drive.

Electrolytes & Labs

Sodium (Na⁺)

135–145 mEq/L

  • Hyponatremia: confusion, seizures, headache, nausea
  • Hypernatremia: thirst, dry mucous membranes, restlessness

Potassium (K⁺)

3.5–5.0 mEq/L

  • Hyperkalemia ECG: peaked T waves, wide QRS
  • Hypokalemia ECG: flattened T waves, U waves
  • Never give IV potassium by push.

Calcium (Ca⁺⁺)

Roughly 8.5–10.5 mg/dL (ranges vary by lab)

  • Hypocalcemia: Chvostek’s sign (facial twitch), Trousseau’s sign (carpal spasm with BP cuff)

Platelets

150,000–400,000/mm³

Bleeding precautions typically below 50,000; spontaneous bleeding risk rises below 20,000.

INR

Normal about 0.8–1.1. Therapeutic on warfarin: 2.0–3.0 (2.5–3.5 for mechanical valves). Antidote: vitamin K.

aPTT

Normal about 30–40 seconds. Therapeutic on heparin: 1.5–2.5× control. Antidote: protamine sulfate.

Note: warfarin is monitored with PT/INR, heparin with aPTT.

Arterial Blood Gases

Normal ABG values

  • pH 7.35–7.45
  • PaCO₂ 35–45 mmHg
  • HCO₃⁻ 22–26 mEq/L
  • PaO₂ 80–100 mmHg

high-yield

ROME mnemonic

Respiratory Opposite, Metabolic Equal.

Respiratory: pH and PaCO₂ move in opposite directions. Metabolic: pH and HCO₃⁻ move in the same direction.
